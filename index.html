01 / About me

Curiosity led me here.

A non-linear path into data, shaped by curiosity, ownership, and a habit of asking the next useful question.

I have been drawn to computers since childhood. What began as curiosity about how technology works grew into a natural strength for learning new systems and finding smarter ways to use them.

Although my academic background is in Arabic Language and Literature, I chose to explore the kind of work I could genuinely grow into before graduation. I started in the medical analysis field as a receptionist and data-entry specialist, working with the laboratory’s system control environment.

My ability to understand the system, spot patterns, and improve the flow of information led to a promotion into managing the System Control department. From there, I moved closer to the questions behind the data, and into the field of data analysis.

Today, I bring a strong analytical mindset, practical problem-solving, careful attention to detail, and the ability to connect data with business context.
